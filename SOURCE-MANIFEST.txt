This archive contains the complete Git-tracked HYLE tree for the commit above:
original C++ source, public C++ headers, build/package files, tests, benchmarks, samples,
documentation, scripts, and in-tree third-party materials.

Local build trees, caches, ignored files, and fetched dependency checkouts are not
part of the HYLE source tree and are not included. Pinned fetched dependencies can
be obtained by CMake or supplied separately through the standard
FETCHCONTENT_SOURCE_DIR_<NAME> inputs for an offline build.
